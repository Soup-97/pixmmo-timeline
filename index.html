    Feature 1 · End of October

    Daily Login Rewards

    Players open the game every day. It's one tap, takes no effort, and only says "welcome back".

    Monthly calendar · try it

    December board

    0 / 28
    Normal day Milestone Monthly cosmetic Claimed
    Demo: each click is one login day.

    How it works

    • A board of 28 days that resets on the 1st of each month.
    • Each day you log in, you hit Claim on the next square and a chest opens.
    • Missing a day is forgiving. Nothing resets; you just don't reach the last squares that month.
    • Unlocks after the tutorial or around level 3, so throwaway alt accounts can't farm it.
    • One global reset time: 00:00 UTC.

    Login streak

    A flame counter for consecutive days, shown on your profile. It's separate from the calendar.

    🔥0day streak
    "Loyal"30 days
    "Devoted"100 days
    "Legend"365 days
    • Cosmetic titles only. No gems from the streak.
    • One streak freeze per week, so a single missed day doesn't wipe out months of streak.

    Rewards

    DaysFree rewardPremium row
    Normal daysGold (scaled to level), HP potions, a small XP scroll, resourcesExtra potions, more gold
    Day 7✨ Rarity Boost potionExtra consumables
    Day 14💎 25 gems + an extra consumableNo gems, extra potions & gold
    Day 21🃏 Pet or mount skin shard / collectable cardExtra consumables
    Day 28👑 Exclusive monthly cosmetic (frame, name effect or banner), different every monthPremium-only cosmetic variant

    💎 Gem limit

    • At most 25 gems per month from login rewards, only on day 14.
    • That's about 5% of the smallest pack. It feels good to get, but it never replaces buying gems.
    • The day-28 cosmetic is the best login reward. Players come back for that, not for gems.

    ★ Premium

    • Premium players get a second reward per day (premium row), like the Battle Pass.
    • The premium row has no gems: only extra potions, more gold and premium-only cosmetic variants.
    • That makes Premium more attractive without giving away free gems.

    ✦ Extras

    • Push notification at milestones: "Your Day 14 chest is waiting!"
    • Calendar items can't be traded, so nobody can farm them on alt accounts and sell them on the Marketplace.
    • A preview of the day-28 reward is visible from day 1, so players know what they're working toward.
    Feature 2 · End of October

    Quests

    Once players are in the game, they get concrete goals for the next 10–20 minutes, and doing them pays off.

    Quest Board
    Travel 300 steps300 / 300 · AFK
    Defeat 15 enemies9 / 15
    Upgrade an item at the Smith0 / 1
    All 3 done → 🎁 bonus chest

    Design rules

    • At least one daily quest finishes passively (travel), since this is an AFK game.
    • Targets scale with level, so the same quest means the same effort at level 5 and level 80.
    • One free reroll per day, for when a quest doesn't fit (for example, a guild quest for a player without a guild).
    • Finishing all 3 daily quests gives a bonus chest.
    • No frustrating quests like "lose 3 fights" or "die once".

    Three quest types

    👣 Travel

    🏟 Arena

    🏰 Dungeon / Raid

    Clear X dungeons, attack the raid boss

    🛡 Guild

    ⚖ Economy

    ⛏ Gathering

    Collect X resources

    ✦ Other

    Quests support sales

    • Only the weekly bonus (all 5 weekly quests done) gives gems: 15 gems per week, about 60 per month.
    • Daily quests never give gems.
    • The main reward is Battle Pass XP. Quests become the best way to level the Battle Pass, which makes the premium Battle Pass more worth buying. Quests support sales instead of competing with them.

    ✦ Extras

    • The Quest Board becomes a new building in town, in pixel-art style and laid out for mobile.
    • A nav badge shows how many rewards are ready to claim.
    • Admin control: quest rewards and gem amounts can be changed in the admin panel without a code update.
    • Later: completed quests add points to the guild's weekly score.
    • Later: event quests during seasonal events like Halloween, with event rewards.
